NHOU Hybrid

NHOU HYBRID is our highest-rated formula for vehicles with little to no rust. It penetrates to the base metal, displaces moisture, and cures within 48 hours into a pliable, matte-black layer that protects against road salt and rust.

We then mist NHOU Clear Oil Undercoating into frame cavities, body panels, and the engine bay. The product contains peppermint, which rodents do not like.

NHOU HYBRID includes a lifetime, transferable warranty.
